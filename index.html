AI-generated executive summary

The project remains on track toward the planned production window, with two items requiring active attention. UAT completion is behind the original internal target, creating schedule risk for final validation. In parallel, carrier readiness for the production voice migration remains a dependency. The team should confirm a committed UAT completion date, validate carrier readiness, maintain a documented rollback path, and escalate any unresolved blocker that threatens the production entry criteria.

Reusable AI prompt

This prompt can be adapted for ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, or another enterprise-approved AI platform.

You are an enterprise project delivery assistant.

Review the project information provided by the project manager.

Produce:
1. Executive status summary
2. Risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies
3. Customer/business impact
4. Action items with owner and timing
5. Escalations requiring leadership attention
6. Go-live readiness assessment

Rules:
- Do not invent facts.
- Separate known facts from recommendations.
- Identify missing information.
- Highlight schedule, customer, technical, operational, and dependency risks.
- Recommend practical next actions.
- Keep the executive summary concise.
- A human project manager must validate AI-generated output before customer or executive distribution.

About this project

This project demonstrates practical experimentation with AI for project delivery rather than treating AI as a replacement for project-management judgment. The scenario is intentionally fictional and designed to show how AI can help organize information, surface potential risks, and reduce administrative work.
